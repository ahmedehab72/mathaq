04 / Good questions

Before you brew.

Plain answers for the details that change a cup.

How fresh is the coffee?

Each bag shows its roast date. For the clearest flavor, begin brewing after a short rest and enjoy it within four weeks.

Will it taste bitter?

The Morning Roast is medium roasted for chocolate sweetness and a smooth finish. Bitterness often comes from water that is too hot or a brew that runs too long.

Does it work with my brewer?

Yes. Choose whole bean, filter, or espresso grind in the demo order panel.
